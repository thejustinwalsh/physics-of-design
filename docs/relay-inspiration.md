The authored **Night instrument** direction uses calm deep housing, clear plotting fields and a small bright action vocabulary. **Day instrument** uses mineral substrate, precise dark ink and a restrained industrial diagram language. Both must support light/dark, the same dense content, the same operations and the same accessible focus/status system. No route is prescribed to resemble a reference site.

For real DOM light/material work, three concrete hypotheses follow: (1) place a broad, slow light field on the instrument housing while keeping dense plot/table interiors opaque; (2) distinguish matte work surfaces, elevated panels and glazed controls through highlight breadth/texture/boundary under one light direction; (3) align a decorative light plane with actual current element geometry and keep semantic content and hit targets stationary. These are authored Relay hypotheses. Awwwards’ 3D examples do not directly establish a DOM shader recipe.

The Tide Guide press image and Igloo creator image were visually inspected from temporary local files, with no numeric extraction or live interaction measurement. Images are linked in the ledger and are not repository assets. The Igloo image explicitly reserves rights. Flighty’s press-image link was verified; Watch Duty and Terminal were researched through source text/case credits. No claim of live app usability, accessibility, composite contrast, native parity or performance is made from those sources.

Do not transfer reference brands, layouts, logos, fonts, maps, meshes, shaders, textures, audio, copy or claimed reliability. In particular, immersive scroll narratives and canvas-only text do not belong in Relay’s incident workflow. Source rights remain protected. No screenshot is a license. Relay’s two technical SVGs and deterministic dataset are original, separately licensed inputs.
