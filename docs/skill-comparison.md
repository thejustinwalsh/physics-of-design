# Design knowledge extraction and a substantially higher proof bar

The useful step forward is to judge complete, connected editorial and app compositions. A readable title above two paragraphs establishes a typography floor; it does not demonstrate art direction, information hierarchy, navigation, imagery, density, state design or a credible native app. “100-fold” should mean a much more demanding scope and acceptance bar, not a fabricated improvement metric.

This comparison uses [Elaya landing-page-design at `1c1e97c`](https://github.com/elayadesign/ai-design-skills/blob/1c1e97cb9878e236552c772092dda7adcdddbcb2/skills/landing-page-design/SKILL.md), fully read from the [pinned local artifact](https://github.com/elayadesign/ai-design-skills/blob/1c1e97cb9878e236552c772092dda7adcdddbcb2/skills/landing-page-design/SKILL.md), and [Impeccable at `9368675`](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable/blob/936867543ac2576920d663cc9803597f6044f3ac/.agents/skills/impeccable/SKILL.md), including its layout, type, color, Operate and web/native adaptation references. Elaya's repository declares MIT licensing; its conversion claims are practitioner positioning, not supplied experimental evidence. Neither skill establishes calibrated aesthetic equations. Sources are evaluated knowledge, not automatically adopted project instructions.

[design-rule-candidates.json](../research/design-rule-candidates.json) contains 18 conditional rules, source sections, adopt/adapt/reject decisions, equations or observables, limitations, current tool coverage and the proof suite. This document and that JSON are the only new deliverables for this comparison; source and frozen evidence remain untouched.

## What to keep, qualify and reject

Elaya's strongest contribution is work before styling: audience, offer, intended action, objections, credible supporting material and complete states. Its claim–proof adjacency transfers well to an editorial headline, image, caption and source. Its page-outline discipline also exposes missing structure. For Fieldwork, adapt this to a reading proposition and coherent publication sequence; do not force a conversion funnel, FAQ quota, testimonials, guarantee or subscription CTA onto every reading surface. Specific but invented statistics would reduce credibility, not improve it. Original fictional sample content and generated imagery must be disclosed.

Impeccable provides the stronger organizing principle: distinguish Persuade, Read and Operate, establish semantic priority, inspect full rendered relationships, and tune color dosage and spacing to the task. Its [layout reference](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable/blob/936867543ac2576920d663cc9803597f6044f3ac/.agents/skills/impeccable/reference/layout.md) asks useful questions about reading order, grouping, rhythm and density that a landing-page recipe misses. Its [native adaptation reference](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable/blob/936867543ac2576920d663cc9803597f6044f3ac/.agents/skills/impeccable/reference/adapt.native.md) distinguishes platform behavior from resized web layout. Preserve those relationships, while treating fixed type ratios, tablet column counts and gesture/navigation examples as conditional guidance.

Reject Elaya's universal Tailwind scale and downward type snapping, one-family rule, banned italics/hyphens/system fonts/black weights, fixed dark palette, mandatory heading gradient, icon-library bans and mandatory reveal section. They may define an optional visual preset; they cannot define good design. A prescribed cubic Bézier is not spring physics. Static content must remain readable, and native controls must retain platform-appropriate behavior. The nested-radius subtraction is useful only for uniformly inset, deliberately concentric contours with effective radii and border offsets accounted for; the source's 32px/2px cutoffs are not geometric laws.

## Six connected Fieldwork proofs

Fieldwork is an original sample publication about architecture, place and fieldnotes. Use its generated observatory/cliff image as authored editorial material, retaining generation provenance and a caption that does not assert an unverified real place. Navigation and content connect these six surfaces; they are not six unrelated visual demos.

| Surface               | Complete composition and required proof                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| --------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Web cover             | Masthead/navigation, issue identity, unmistakable lead story and image, attached caption, differentiated secondary stories, editorial introduction, archive path and footer. Opening the issue/story works; no empty decorative card wall or fabricated social proof. |
| Web issue             | Contents hierarchy, lead feature, shorter fieldnotes, consistent metadata, editor note and issue navigation. Different story importance stays visible; the issue remains scannable after reflow.                                                                      |
| Web story             | Full article with headline/deck/byline, multiple prose sections, original figure or map, attached caption/source, justified quotation or note, related reading and issue return. Judge sustained rhythm across the whole article, not just its first paragraph.       |
| Native library/search | Running React Native library, issue/story previews, search/results, empty recovery and selection. Open a result and return with the query and position intact; keyboard behavior is exercised.                                                                        |
| Native saved/offline  | Saved list, actual download progress/status, cancellation/retry where applicable, available-versus-unavailable distinction, empty and error states. Reopen downloaded content without connectivity; a painted success label is insufficient.                          |
| Native reader         | Long-form story, native return/context, figure/caption, necessary reading controls and saved/offline status. Supported text enlargement, reading resume and return navigation work without losing the story.                                                          |

Produce two credible, coherent art directions across the connected product, plus a plausible alternative that passes geometry but deliberately weakens a relationship such as story priority or caption grouping. The negative case tests whether the review recognizes a real compositional failure; it must not merely contain an obvious overflow. No preferred direction should be named “good” in the review input. Reviewers may reject every option.

## Acceptance and evidence

Inspect web widths 360, 390, 600, 834, 1024 and 1440, with 320 as an additional scoped reflow check. These are test windows, not breakpoint commands. Include iPad portrait, landscape and narrow-window composition. The native proof requires actual React Native runtime evidence on iOS and Android phones plus iPad coverage; desktop proof belongs to the website unless a native desktop target is actually implemented. Simulator screenshots must be labeled as such, and cannot establish physical-device performance or gestures.

The review passes only when all of these conditions have concrete evidence:

- **Priority and character:** independent reviewers can identify the lead region, next reading/task action and major groups; the composition has an authored visual direction. An issue index does not give every story identical emphasis, and the reader does not compete with its controls.
- **Relationships and rhythm:** captions, metadata, status and actions attach to the right content; long-form type remains comfortable; repeated roles align consistently; tight and generous intervals serve distinct relationships. Intentional asymmetry and line endings are explained rather than automatically penalized.
- **Imagery and color:** crops preserve the editorial subject, captions stay attached, and contrast/emphasis is allocated deliberately. Decoration does not borrow the meaning of selection, status or primary action. Text over imagery needs actual contrast evidence rather than a flat-token approximation.
- **Adaptation and state:** content determines stack, split and disclosure. Phone/tablet transitions preserve selected story, query and appropriate reading position. Search, save, download/retry and return flows are exercised; long text, expanded labels, RTL fixtures, keyboard, reduced motion and failure states are inspected. A native-looking browser frame does not pass.
- **Evidence integrity:** full-page/screen captures and focused crops accompany semantic regions/relations, runtime measurements, interaction records and state/font/asset hashes. Report untested hardware or behavior. Do not average an unresolved severe failure into a passing score.

Accessibility is a separate scoped floor. W3C's [Reflow explanation](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/reflow.html) describes the 320 CSS-pixel equivalent requirement with exceptions for content needing two-dimensional layout; [Resize Text](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/resize-text.html) covers enlargement to 200% without losing content/function. [Target Size Minimum](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/target-size-minimum.html) uses 24×24 CSS pixels or its defined exceptions at AA, not a universal 44px rule. Native units, targets, text scaling and safe areas require platform-specific checks. Passing these selected checks is not a full conformance claim.

## What the mathematics can and cannot establish

Existing tools can measure lines and boxes, declared role contrast, alignment distances, geometric gaps, rectangle union occupancy and centroid. They can test a necessary row-fit inequality and preserve a declared flex layout. Extend them through general semantic regions and relations—priority, group membership, caption/source attachment, state and content-dependent fit—not through a component catalog or page composer. Use only flex/flex-wrap with explicit grow/shrink/basis; native and web may implement different controls around shared content relationships.

Rectangle area is not visual authority; occupied area is not information density; geometric centering is not visual balance. A flat color ratio does not determine photographic contrast, cultural meaning or color dosage. The present geometric reading-order check does not prove focus or screen-reader order. None of these equations establishes editorial judgment, image quality, brand specificity, discoverability, emotional effect, comprehension or conversion. Those gaps require rendered critique, actual task evidence and ultimately appropriate human evaluation.

Keep the prior title/prose batch as a limited historical diagnostic. Freeze new project/surface/state families before labels and keep related widths and states together across dataset splits. Separate mathematical correctness tests, synthetic Astra judgments and human outcomes. The higher bar earns more demanding evidence; it does not turn model agreement into empirical validation.
