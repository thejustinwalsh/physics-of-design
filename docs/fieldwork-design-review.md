Focal cropping preserves the observatory in the reviewed hero views. Reusing the same landscape for different stories still weakens story recognition, even when crop arithmetic is correct. That is a local editorial decision: use a distinct authored image, detail or text-led guide treatment. It should not become a universal penalty for repeated image hashes. **Native quality and platform fit.** The iPad app has a useful distinction from the website: stable collection navigation, a search input, saved-state text, compact story rows and a focused reader. Default text is readable in the supplied capture. Sparse Saved content honestly represents one saved story; its empty space is not a defect to decorate away. Save/check icons are visually less specific than a consistent bookmark state; keep saved state distinct from offline readiness and completion, and verify spoken labels. The source's removal label helps, but pixels alone do not demonstrate comprehension.

Apple's guidance makes navigation choice dependent on available space and the content task. A permanent sidebar is not automatically superior on an iPad; a tab bar can preserve more room, and a hideable sidebar can help focused reading. Here the sidebar is usable at 834, but the reader should be tested with a content-focused mode and narrow iPad windows before this becomes a universal toolkit rule. Do not imitate operating-system chrome or glass merely to claim native quality. [Apple HIG: Sidebars](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/sidebars), [Navigation and search](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/navigation-and-search).

Custom serif/sans typography is compatible with a publication app. It needs real text enlargement and reflow, legible weights, and preserved control/content relationships. A declaration that scaling is enabled is not the same as reviewing enlarged native screens. Rotation, split view and longer localized labels must preserve task state as well as fit. [Apple HIG: Typography](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/typography), [Layout](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/layout).
