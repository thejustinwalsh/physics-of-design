## Implementation evidence categories

- **Measurement:** actual font metadata, glyph positions, primary glyph coverage, renderer lines/rectangles, wrap brackets, native events and runtime identity.
- **Standard:** scoped text-spacing override tolerance in applicable web specimens.
- **Empirical:** published studies/reviews, with their populations and domains retained in the registry.
- **Heuristic:** smooth targets, density neighborhoods, tracking interpolation, leading/measure/gap suggestions and bounded-search budgets. Their numbers were chosen for this spike and have no fitted confidence interval.
- **Hypothesis:** musical hierarchy landmarks, visual interval expectations and any claimed preference advantage. Disabled by default.

No licensed award corpus has been assembled and no aesthetic compatibility curve is calibrated. A six-record original CC0 pilot and six-pair synthetic Astra batch exercise corpus/export mechanics. `research/corpus-record.schema.json` requires attribution, permissions, font licenses, genre/audience/year/platform strata, matched production controls and project-level split identity. Keep all perturbations/viewports of a source project in one split. Screenshot-only inference cannot supply font metrics or responsive rules.

## Design Theory: coupled motion, materials and information

Checked2026-10-25. Product naming changed; the earlier paper and experiments retain their historical names.

- [Disney/Burley2012](https://disneyanimation.com/publications/physically-based-shading-at-disney/) starts from measured reflectance observations and preserves artistic control. [Filament](https://google.github.io/filament/main/filament.html) supplies explicit material, energy, parameterization and lighting conventions. These support a principled material model, not a pleasing-pair preference equation. The toolkit names its single-layer Lambert/GGX approximation and omissions.
- [Norman, Todd and Phillips2019](https://arxiv.org/abs/1908.00902) experimentally shows illumination changing shiny-material categorization. [Filip et al. Material Fingerprinting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.13615) measures perceptual attributes from dynamic material stimuli (the arXiv record also identifies a2025 journal publication). Neither establishes optimal UI material pairings. New pairing tools therefore expose separate descriptors, authored bands and Pareto alternatives; empirical preference and actual text-over-material contrast remain unknown.
- [PBRT sampling theory](https://pbr-book.org/4ed/Sampling_and_Reconstruction/Sampling_Theory) informs explicit texture-frequency/sampling descriptors. A declared frequency below Nyquist does not certify a perspective-rendered, harmonically rich normal map. Real filters and final pixels remain relevant.
- [GSAP documentation](https://gsap.com/docs/v3/), [Lenis](https://github.com/darkroomengineering/lenis) and [anime.js documentation](https://animejs.com/documentation/) establish APIs and runtime options. No universal GSAP-versus-anime speed ranking follows. The examples use pinned GSAP3.15.0, Lenis1.3.27 and Three0.186.1. GSAP has its own standard no-charge license; Lenis and Three retain MIT. Upstream licenses remain distinct from this project's MIT terms.
- [W3C orientation](https://www.w3.org/TR/orientation-event/) and [Apple Safari17 release notes](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/safari-release-notes/safari-17-release-notes) inform permission, coordinates and worker capability handling. Local Chromium and Playwright WebKit support probes are port/runtime evidence; physical Safari sensor, thermal, power and native tilt behavior remain unverified. See the scoped [motion/Safari research](motion-safari-research.md).
- [Diátaxis](https://diataxis.fr/map/) distinguishes reader purposes. [Attention Control](https://github.com/aaddrick/attention-control) supplies practitioner organization practices, with upstream attribution preserved in [prose research](prose-organization.md). Neither validates literary beauty, a clinical intervention or a universal controlled vocabulary. Explicit reader journey probabilities and cost assumptions support navigational calculations; no inferred distribution is substituted.

The new validation state machine coordinates explicit scenario and tool obligations with current receipt identities. It does not discover every responsive permutation, execute the validators or authenticate caller-supplied evidence.
