## Concrete findings

These findings describe the captured baseline. Corrections were independently rechecked as recorded below; source changes alone were not treated as verification.

1. **P1 — edited motion does not survive export.** Set Motion damping to 35 in Editorial, then choose Download motion.json. The browser downloads damping **13**. `MotionDemo` holds local damping while `Workflow` exports `profile.motion`. This contradicts the workflow's promise to carry chosen inputs forward. [Actual payload](../evidence/design-theory-2026-10-25/design-theory/astra-review/interactions.json), [edited demo](../evidence/design-theory-2026-10-25/design-theory/astra-review/edited-motion.png), [source](../examples/design-theory/web.jsx). Use one serializable decision state for controls, analysis, export and replay; test round-trip equivalence after changing a nondefault value. This is a shared state-contract correction, not a copy adjustment.
2. **P2 — the design and drawing disagree about mobile composition.** Choose Phone · 390 in Whole compositions. Design places the action above the image; Inspect relationships places it below. The design also derives padding and gap through separate 0.65/0.7 multipliers instead of its resolved nodes. [Design](../evidence/design-theory-2026-10-25/design-theory/astra-review/phone-layout-design.png), [drawing](../evidence/design-theory-2026-10-25/design-theory/astra-review/phone-layout-inspect.png). The proposed-evidence disclaimer correctly disclaims browser measurement, but does not explain this semantic difference. Derive both representations from the same node order, geometry and breakpoint decisions. A rendered receipt should compare actual boxes to that proposal separately.
3. **P2 — the phone drawing loses its top above the scroll origin.** At scrollTop 0, the drawing host is 520px tall, the SVG is 800px tall, and its top is **140px above** the host. The host's scrollHeight is 672px, so scrolling cannot recover that negative region. [Measured bounds](../evidence/design-theory-2026-10-25/design-theory/astra-review/interactions.json). A bounded scroll presentation must keep the graph's origin reachable; assert that all proposed nodes are reachable by scrolling, independently of whole-page overflow checks.
4. **P2 — initial reduced-motion hero can be blank despite draw counters.** In an isolated Chromium run at 320×900 and 834×900, navigate, wait for the gallery ready flag and fonts, then wait 1.5 seconds: the circular frame is empty. Clicking the current Editorial profile and returning to the top restores the shapes. [Initial 320](../evidence/design-theory-2026-10-25/design-theory/astra-review/hero-initial-320.png), [returned 320](../evidence/design-theory-2026-10-25/design-theory/astra-review/hero-return-320.png), [initial 834](../evidence/design-theory-2026-10-25/design-theory/astra-review/hero-initial-834.png), [reproduction](../evidence/design-theory-2026-10-25/design-theory/astra-review/hero-return.mjs). Root cause is not established. A worker ready flag and recorded draw call do not establish visible pixels after sizing/compositing. Retain a visible fallback until a valid presentation, and verify initial/re-entry frames under reduced motion without relying only on counters.

## Material and image integrity

The material demo uses actual Three `MeshStandardMaterial`, linear-sRGB base-color parameters, separate roughness maps with scalar roughness set to 1, normal maps, metalness endpoints for the initial authored profiles, environment lighting and two directional lights. Intermediate metalness is explicitly described as a blend/transition. The model inspector distinguishes the directional GGX/Schlick/Smith calculation from the environment-lit, tone-mapped scene; it does not claim nits or Three pixel parity. The phrase “Two surfaces, one light” would be more exact as “one lighting rig.”

The paired descriptors are correctly labeled as parameter differences and an authored policy. They are not demonstrated preference predictors. This interpretation follows the distinctions documented with primary sources in [material pairings](material-pairings.md): Disney/Filament/Khronos establish a shading model; material-perception work motivates controlling illumination and shape, not assigning a universal nice-pair score.

The gallery's pair analysis currently supplies surface and rig provenance but no texture metadata. Its normal-scale and relief controls affect rendering without participating in texture-frequency/amplitude descriptors. That is an integration limit, not proof of a false reported texture result. A reusable improvement is to carry explicit texture recipe, resolution and UV-repeat metadata into the decision state. Normal-map strength must remain distinct from physical height amplitude; unknown physical scale should remain unknown. Compare profile-specific continuity/counterpoint bands with `materialPairCandidates`, preserving unresolved candidates, then render the retained alternatives under the same rig. Do not manufacture an aggregate aesthetic score.

The original still-life SVG supports authored crop and composition exercises. Its focal marker is an authored choice, and the gallery does not claim object detection. It does not by itself demonstrate raster-photo analysis, source-image contrast bounds or calibrated albedo extraction. The separate [surface/image module](material-image-rules.md) and original Fieldwork raster evidence cover that different scope. No image was imported from an award site during this review.

## Validation phase-gate review

Read-only review of `src/validation-machine.ts` and its tests examined the **coordination boundary**, not the truth of caller-supplied evidence. The plan/design/input/tool/evidence-kind comparisons, duplicate/unknown-requirement rejection and explicit unsupported policy are appropriate for that boundary. `RECEIPTS` events restart phase checks even after completion; changing the scenario changes the plan hash. The synchronous `checkValidation` path replays ordered guards and does not infer missing tool results.

**P1 — the live actor exposes mutable receipt state that can bypass restarting earlier guards.** This was reproduced with a two-phase fixture: start with passes, advance to phase1, set `actor.getSnapshot().context.receipts[0].outcome = 'fail'`, then advance. The actor reports `complete`, while `inspect(actor.getSnapshot().context.receipts)` reports readiness `[false, true]`. The existing returned-plan clone test does not protect context receipt objects. The snapshot's mutation does not pass through the `RECEIPTS` transition, and the next guard checks only its own phase.

Freeze or otherwise protect the stored receipt array and its records at both construction and replacement; consider checking all predecessor obligations at advance as an additional invariant. Test mutation after advancing and after completion, and test replacement through the supported event. This is an in-process API integrity issue. It does not mean a passing receipt was authenticated, nor does it demonstrate a remote exploit or a failure of the bounded synchronous CLI path. Root was given the exact reproduction before making any source correction.

### Validation finding closure

**Closed by independent reproduction after the correction.** Context, receipt array and receipt records now reject direct mutation; replacement receipts are copied, so mutating the caller's array after sending cannot change actor evidence. A supported replacement containing a prior failure restarts phase0 and blocks progress. An invalid update after completion clears old evidence, records the error and leaves the actor active at phase0; subsequent valid evidence clears the error and permits ordered completion. The original snapshot-mutation reproduction now throws `TypeError` and cannot change the stored pass.

An independent inline Node/tsx probe checked those paths, including completion reset and recovery. All **11** current `test/validation-machine.test.ts` tests also passed. The scope remains a coordination guarantee over declarations: neither freezing nor state progression authenticates the referenced evidence or converts synthetic judgment into factual/human verification.

## Independent gallery follow-up

The second focused run in [postfix-final/report.json](../evidence/design-theory-2026-10-25/design-theory/astra-review/postfix-final/report.json) independently closes the export/replay and drawing findings. The preview and technical drawing agree in twelve cases: actual host widths 320 and 1440, proposed widths 390/834/960, and default/extreme gutter/gap values. Maximum normalized geometry error is **0.178 authored px**. Graph origin and footer are reachable. The first correction exposed a real content-fit regression: a 190px phone title box contained 196.78125px of natural content. The owner replaced that fixed-height assumption with measurement of the same loaded-font content at the resolved column width, using `titleHeight = max(authoredMinimum, ceil(naturalTextHeight))`; downstream image/action/footer positions derive from it. Line heights now scale with the preview. The independent twelve-case rerun finds no title/child overflow, without relying on hidden overflow to mask text. [Corrected preview](../evidence/design-theory-2026-10-25/design-theory/astra-review/postfix-final/phone-design.png).

The initial-frame finding is **not yet closed** by this follow-up. The 834px initial scene contains visible materials, but the 320px capture is blank. Its worker reported a completed render and one requested frame; the current `lastRenderMilliseconds` field also accepts initialization frame messages with no frame ID, so this does not prove the requested frame's matching acknowledgment. A subsequent check must synchronize to an explicit acknowledged frame ID rather than treating any render counter as presentation evidence. The initial fixed-delay probe also captured a legitimate static fallback during initialization; that fallback is not counted as a blank-scene failure. Likewise, the workflow requires explicit Replay after reload, rather than automatic application of saved state. Those probe assumptions were corrected in the focused rerun.
