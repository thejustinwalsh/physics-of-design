# Vela — make room for more

An original, local-first training journal for people who want purposeful movement. A complete native app and responsive web companion demonstrate how a resolved mathematical design policy becomes a working product.

The visual world is precise and energetic: ink surfaces, electric lime for commitment, cobalt for a held session, warm white typography, oversized editorial numerals, diagonal interval traces, asymmetric hierarchy and generous semantic grouping. Movement has anticipation, a held active state and a clear release. Tension is intentional. Controls stay legible and predictable.

Today presents one primary session and a recovery/check-in summary. Session detail explains the interval phases. Start enters a running timer with pause, resume and finish. Completion records the local session and leads back to Today or Insights. Insights separates sample history from local completed sessions. Profile includes appearance and motion settings. This is a training journal, with no diagnosis or health scoring authority.

Phone is the primary product. iPad and desktop use a navigation rail and paired content where the measured pane and text scale can support them. All layouts use flex with natural text heights. Font scaling remains enabled. Every interactive target has at least a 44pt proposed iOS hit area. Reduced motion replaces spatial page movement with a short opacity transition or an immediate state change.

The shared mathematical program owns display/body hierarchy, size-dependent tracking, density and grouping, gutters, surface radius and spring parameters. Perceptual color tools check declared foreground/surface roles. Actual renderers remain responsible for text wrapping, clipping, safe areas and final geometry. The app consumes the generated contract; implementation must not quietly replace the equations with screen-specific patches.

Acceptance requires real navigation through the session flow, responsive and enlarged-text specimens, observable reduced motion, and an independent visual critique. A mathematically admissible proposal is a starting point for that verification, not a claim of award-winning quality.
