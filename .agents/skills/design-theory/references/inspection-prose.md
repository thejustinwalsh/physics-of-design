# Inspect equations and information architecture

Use `wireframe inspect` through the CLI or `design_specimen` to inspect whole-layout technical drawings. Proposed geometry is dashed; caller-supplied renderer receipts are solid and fixed. Edits recompute proposals and share exact variable changes/node identities through the host. Never call a proposal edit a newly measured rectangle. `component inspect` retains precise component anatomy.

Use `prose analyze` for section facts, authored terminology, navigation/prerequisite structure and explicit reader journeys. Diátaxis distinguishes reader purposes; it does not force every page into four folders. Preserve expressive copy. Attention-control practices inform action placement and controlled terms; do not impose a diagnostic assumption or a universal word blacklist.

Reading/click costs are authored assumptions, not measured cognition. Journey entropy requires an explicit complete normalized distribution; unknown probabilities remain unknown. Changing navigation can alter route cost without changing word count or entropy. No prose-beauty or cognitive-load score is implied. Source and scope: docs/prose-organization.md and docs/wireframe-inspection.md.
