Start from the product brief, real content, art direction and licensed assets. Interview only material gaps. Preserve the caller’s framework and style; energy, tension, physics and musical intervals are optional vocabulary, not a mandatory aesthetic.

Discover one command schema with `design_catalog`, then use `design_execute` summaries and exact session references. The CLI emits full composable JSON. React + StyleX and React Native use flex; shared constraints do not establish renderer parity.

Keep facts, hard constraints, attributed practices, brief preferences and hypotheses separate. No universal beauty score, entropy objective or model-confidence verification. Preserve multiple alternatives and revisable values. Measure actual browser/native finalists after changes; edited previews and mathematical admissibility are not current renderer receipts.

Load only the relevant reference:

- [Typography precision](references/typography.md): real baselines, variation-axis probes and renderer-specific text evidence.
- [Composition](references/composition.md): product surfaces, semantic priorities and responsive topology.
- [Decision paths](references/design-machine.md): bounded numeric search, staged local selection and cached reuse.
- [Motion and materials](references/motion-materials.md): driven springs, scroll, tilt, PBR, textures and conditional material relationships.
- [Human inspection and prose](references/inspection-prose.md): layout technical drawings, exact human edits and task-oriented information architecture.

Phone, iPad/narrow-window and desktop are distinct test scenarios. Test user enlargement, content changes, real task flows, reduced motion and unsupported features. Award references inspire art direction; they do not grant asset rights or prove causal design rules. Synthetic Astra critique stays labeled synthetic. Current measured limits and open quality claims belong in the result.
