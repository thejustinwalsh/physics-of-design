# Design Theory — Forces become form

## Intent and audience
A visual launch page for developers and designers working with coding agents. Lead with a simple promise: design choices can work together, be checked and stay editable. Curiosity first, technical depth on demand.

## Art direction
Modern experimental editorial studio meets a physical design laboratory. Bold, energetic, sophisticated and a little risky. Large expressive typography, unexpected composition, purposeful negative space and convincing material/imagery detail. Use the supplied sculpture campaign artwork prominently; it embodies the tension between rough stone, lacquer, metal and glass. Develop a coherent palette from warm paper, dark ink and vivid accents; no default teal/cyan dashboard. You can choose the exact palette and composition. Avoid generic SaaS gradient blobs, endless identical cards and tiny technical labels carrying the story.

## Narrative and information
One page, one coherent visual world. Hero → capabilities illustrated through form → coupled-choice demonstration → simple workflow → get started. CONTENT.md provides factual content, not a fixed component layout. Keep ordinary visitor copy free of internal benchmark terminology or instrumentation.

## Type, imagery and surfaces
Use the supplied fonts flexibly, including a variable face if helpful. Typography should supply hierarchy and character. Maintain readable prose and natural heading wrapping. Compose campaign imagery with intentional crops/focal points, supported by actual vector/procedural imagery if useful. Keep texture, diffuse body and restrained highlights visible without reducing text legibility. Images must align intentionally at phone widths.

## Motion and responsive behavior
Single-page parallax with multiple depth layers and visible scroll-linked changes, including a meaningful middle-page visual transformation. Pair energetic moments with quiet reading. Animate light/material response when useful; keep interaction targets stationary. Treat phone, tablet and desktop as distinct compositions with shared narrative. Reduced motion preserves the complete page in a calm form. No configured viewport bounds and no forced scroll trapping.

## Scope and uncertainty
These creative decisions are authored assumptions for this study. They are not evidence that a palette, ratio, material pairing or animation is aesthetically optimal. Shared test requirements are in BRIEF.md. Brand name remains Design Theory; physics and energy are useful explanatory language.
