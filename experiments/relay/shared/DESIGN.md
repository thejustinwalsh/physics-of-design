# Relay — operational precision with material character

## Intent and evidence

Relay serves working on-call engineers. Urgency belongs to a real incident, chart deviation or focused action; atmosphere supplies identity without competing with those signals. This is an authored direction for this product, not a toolkit style rule or a promise of awards. The [recorded brief](BRIEF.md) and [primary inspiration ledger](../../../docs/relay-inspiration.md) are equal inputs for all three builders. There was no human interview. Source facts, authored preferences, mathematical proposals, synthetic critique and renderer observations remain separate.

## Two directions to explore

Compare **Night instrument** (deep ink enclosure, clear cool plotting fields, electric cyan information, restrained warm critical signals) and **Day instrument** (chalk/mineral substrate, dark precise ink, cool glazed controls, sparse technical color). Each needs light and dark modes with coherent role mapping. The directions are alternatives for Relay, not separate required layouts and not copies of reference brands. Use the same functional hierarchy in both finalists. Select a direction with the staged tool workflow and actual renderer evidence; a model selection remains a revisable preference decision.

## Composition and density

Make the first viewport answer “What changed, where, and who owns it?” Give Overview one strong incident/latency narrative, then aligned chart comparisons and a useful service/incident index. Expose actual operations immediately. Service and trace detail deserve enough room to inspect relationships. Use subtle asymmetry in summary versus investigation regions, occasional large operational numbers, strong alignment and disciplined voids to make density legible. Do not add a marketing hero above the tool.

Desktop: persistent navigation rail, stable global context, compact workspace, optional detail pane. iPad/narrow window: collapsible navigation with list/detail only when both retain usable measure; otherwise route to detail and restore context on Back. Phone: compact destination navigation, focused summary/filter sheet and stacked charts, then a readable prioritized row/card representation with explicit expansion. Core actions stay reachable above long history. Columns may move into detail, but data remains available. Breakpoints follow measured content fit, not device-name magic. Natural text height and structural flex reflow survive enlargement and changed strings.

## Typography

Use supplied licensed fonts: Roboto Flex for workhorse UI, optional Lato for a contrasting heading voice, Spectral only if a genuinely useful reading role emerges. Reference paths and licenses are in assets/provenance.json; do not copy a reference brand’s font. Inspect real bytes/features/coverage and every available Roboto Flex axis before selecting coordinates. Compare axes that materially improve small-label texture, heading voice, dense table width or optical rhythm; keep all axes editable in inspection. Do not assume native axis support equals browser support. Tabular numerals and aligned units are required for changing metrics; confirm the font actually offers/enables the feature. Test IDs, error labels, timestamps, punctuation and changed text. Fontkit shaping, browser fragments/baselines and native text geometry are separate evidence.

Author distinct type roles for navigation, page title, section label, metric value, table header/cell, chart axis, tooltip and audit prose. Compact table text must remain readable; size/weight/tracking/leading/measure and row anatomy are coupled decisions. A larger metric number should not erase its unit or comparison. Repeated gap patterns can establish rhythm and one intentional break can separate incident work from routine inventory. Musical ratios are optional hypotheses, never universal pleasing equations or kerning rules.

## Functional color and material roles

Build an OKLCH role palette with primary/secondary text, neutral surfaces, boundaries, focus, selection, normal data, comparison series, warning, critical and recovery. Status always includes wording/icon/shape; hue is not its only carrier. Reserve warning/critical channels for operational state. Chart series, selected rows and keyboard focus must remain distinguishable from incident severity. Test adjacent states and actual composite contrast in both themes and light poses, not only swatches.

Use at least three intentionally different finishes under one shared authored lighting regime: a matte/mineral working substrate, a quieter elevated instrument panel, and a restrained glazed/enamel active control or incident action. A satin/brushed navigation/context strip may replace one role when justified. Material identity comes from diffuse response, highlight breadth/direction, boundary, roughness/texture scale, contact/elevation and use, rather than putting the same gloss gradient on every rounded rectangle. Avoid fine noisy texture behind small data; low-amplitude broad structure belongs on housing and margins. Charts and dense tables have a stable opaque readability layer. Critical status must remain equally clear at every light position.

Lighting follows the real content surface and its current geometry. Pointer-driven light is bounded, low-frequency and secondary; controls do not move under a pointer. Coordinate the optional GPU plane with actual DOM surface/clip/bounds and current input identity on web; native requires a proven native rendering adapter or an explicit static finish. Unsupported shader/blur/anisotropy/refraction stays unsupported. CSS layered finishes are appearance approximations, not measured PBR transport. A decorative 3D icon/object can demonstrate the supplied material convention only if it serves service-topology or status context, initializes without blocking work and has an equivalent static asset. No GPU background is needed to complete a task.

## Charts and technical media

Use supplied data and an explicit time axis; no unlabeled decorative sparklines as the only metric view. Tick density follows available width and label geometry. Maintain a common time scale/shared cursor across visible charts, distinguish missing data from zero, label threshold policies, use useful tick precision and do not truncate axes deceptively. Tooltip text includes bucket interval, series value/unit and comparison. Error/latency overlays use line patterns or labels as well as color. Waterfall intervals, parent spans and selection come from actual span records. The original SVG assets are diagrams of the fixture, not screenshots of the UI; render technical captions and native alternatives. `media analyze`/crops must actually affect a diagram’s placement or variant choice if used; do not claim image analysis of untouched assets.

## Motion and transitions

Motion explains continuity: shared cursor movement, sheet/detail entry, retained list selection, state confirmation and loading-to-content continuity. Animate local highlights and short panel transforms with one managed clock; never animate every numeric cell or reveal the page’s operational controls late. A retargetable spring may drive cursor/selection or an action response; choose damping/settling behavior from measured candidates. Scroll stays native/normal in lists and tables; any web scroll adapter is scoped to decorative context and cannot hijack native app navigation. Reduced motion supplies immediate state changes and static lighting with every task intact. Dispose tickers, observers, sensors, renderers and event listeners on route/unmount; suspend hidden/offscreen work. Device tilt is optional and permission follows an explicit gesture.

## Inspection and tokens

Emit editable role tokens for typography, density, regions, chart geometry, palette, component anatomy, motion, finishes and light. A screenshot/CSS dump is not the token source. Technical inspection selects a current element by stable `relay-*` surface ID and shows actual native or DOM rectangles, measured text/ink where available, proposed geometry separately, active data/brief/recipe identities, inputs, units, provenance, related factor effects and unsupported values. Do not attach an overlay to a stale clone or detached diagram. Inspecting a control must not change its hit geometry. Changed text, theme, size or recipe invalidates affected receipts and preserves unrelated reusable measurements.
