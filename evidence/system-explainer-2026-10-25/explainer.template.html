Hard constraints decide eligibility. Preferences express tradeoffs.

A teaching brief prefers both quiet 24–32px titles and bold 56–64px titles. Those targets conflict. The cap is a separate hard condition. The targets are authored examples, not typographic laws.

TitleQuiet lossBold lossTotalEligibility / Pareto

Loss is weight × squared distance outside a target band, normalized by an 8px scale. The total ranks the authored tradeoff. Pareto means no alternative is better on one preference without being worse on another. Hard failure remains a rejection regardless of the total.
