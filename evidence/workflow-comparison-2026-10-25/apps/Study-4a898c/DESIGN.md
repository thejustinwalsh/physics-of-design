# Design Theory — deliberate friction

## Intent and provenance

This is the visual contract for the developer gallery, not a mandatory style for toolkit output. The user requested a bold, energetic, risky design using motion, DOM light and scroll synchronization. Detailed art direction comes from the [recorded synthetic Astra interview](research/bold-brief-interview.json); its answers are revisable assumptions, not human approval or preference data.

The audience is developers and art directors who want expressive design with inspectable reasoning. The primary action is to open the executable design field, compare an alternative, inspect consequences, and keep an editable result. Do not fabricate awards, adoption, testimonials, time savings or model competence.

## Composition and typography

“Make the rules. Then bend them.” leads. Use oversized, asymmetrical editorial type and sharp changes of scale. Operative controls remain precise and stable. Preserve real copy, natural text height, user enlargement and semantic reading order. Typeface, axes, tracking, leading, measure and gaps are revisable relationships; renderer measurements establish wrapping and containment.

## Color and materials

Near-black ink, warm off-white substrate and an acidic signal color form one direction; cobalt enamel and cool silver offer counterpoint. Colors must pass current role contrast checks. These are authored directions, not universal palette laws.

Light belongs on actual DOM surfaces as well as the 3D study. Matte paper, enamel and brushed finishes should respond differently. CSS gradients and shadows are bounded appearance approximations, not PBR or measured radiometry. Readable content uses a stable shield; nominal token contrast alone cannot certify a moving composite. Avoid glass on glass and decorative material layers that obscure function.

## Responsive behavior

Phone: title and primary action lead; controls precede a single preview and inspection follows. iPad: split controls/preview only when both retain useful measures. Desktop: asymmetric composition with adjacent operative inspection. Use flex and structural reflow. Preserve edits across scenario switches and enlarged text. No interaction may move its own slider rail through dependent layout changes.

## Motion, light and scroll

Use one clock, retained updates and bounded work. Scroll can advance a registration strip and a material-light phase; it must not move paragraphs or controls under the pointer. Pointer light illuminates the content surface. Spring motion is one interaction response; design energy represents competing authored relationships rather than a requirement to animate springs everywhere.

Reduced motion preserves all content, tasks and evidence with static finishes and immediate state changes. Tilt is optional. Stop hidden/offscreen work and clean up listeners, observers and renderers. Do not infer physical Safari performance from Playwright WebKit.

## Inspection and evidence

Inspect the actual rendered composition. Selection shows current observed geometry beside proposed geometry, equations, inputs, source and scenario identity. Unknowns stay unknown. Export a current receipt and editable inputs. A detached diagram, a saved preset or an explanatory workflow tab must not be described as a solver run.

Every demonstrated choice must be traceable to authored input, numeric exploration, local model selection, renderer observation or replay. Record actual calls and hashes. Keep numerical candidate count, neural calls and revalidations distinct. New inputs invalidate affected receipts and choices.

## Acceptance and open questions

Exercise real continuous pointer drags, keyboard, every action, save/replay, downloads, phone/iPad/desktop, changed text and reduced motion. Controls must retain pointer contact; previews and measured overlays must agree. Preserve failing evidence before a correction.

Human preference, award quality and end-to-end token advantage remain unproven. Compare skills-only and tool-assisted builds using the same brief, assets, model and feedback policy; keep synthetic critique separate from measurements. Deliberate asymmetry is welcome. Clipped content, invented evidence and inert controls are defects.
